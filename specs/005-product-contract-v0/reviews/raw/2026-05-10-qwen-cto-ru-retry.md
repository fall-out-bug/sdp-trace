**Strongest reason to proceed**:
The contract finally creates a hard, buyer-readable artifact shape with preserved uncertainty states (`not_assessed`, `cannot_verify`) instead of collapsing everything into a score. The evidence theater framework with named reason codes (`agent_claimed_verification`, `ci_theater`, etc.) is the single most defensible product differentiator - it turns "green CI but missing evidence" into a formal finding rather than a silent gap. This is procurement-ready in concept.

**Strongest reason not to proceed**:
The example packet is still a hypothetical template - not generated from code, not backed by a schema, and not demonstrated with a realistic local-only Russian enterprise fixture. A pilot customer receiving this packet today would see a document that says `not_assessed` / `cannot_verify` in most rows without clear guidance on how to fill them using their existing (non-GitHub, non-signing, non-harness) infrastructure. The contract gates implementation, but the example does not yet prove the gate produces a useful artifact under real baseline constraints.
