## Verdict

`APPROVE_FOR_USER_APPROVAL`

The reviewed direction is now good enough to bring back to the user for explicit
Product Contract v0 approval before implementation. The P0 Classification Rule
is no longer just row citation: it requires row ids, evidence surface,
start-state, target transition, buyer effect, and non-goal. Repeating
`not_assessed -> not_assessed` or `cannot_verify -> cannot_verify` without new
evidence, narrower claim, or explicit unsupported state does not count.

The major review risks after the reshuffled panel were:

- `packet_state` could become a hidden global trust/readiness score.
- `PC-DECISION` owner state could be read as merge/release approval.
- Russian enterprise baseline could look too heavy or too formal for local
  Git/internal CI deployments.

All three are fixed and accepted by focused re-review.
