### ISSUE 1 — Roadmap status contradicts spec source-of-truth status for every historical spec (P0 — schema drift)

**Files:** `docs/roadmap.md` lines 46–56 vs. `specs/002..007/spec.md`

The roadmap marks specs 002–007 as `historical`. Their own `spec.md` files all say `Draft` (with various qualification text). The taxonomy defined in this slice's own legend (line 12) says `draft` = "Spec written, not yet reviewed or approved for implementation." These are contradictory lifecycle states. The roadmap is overwriting the author's own status without changing the source files.

Spec 001's `spec.md` says `Draft`; the roadmap says `blocked`. Spec 008's `spec.md` says `Implemented locally; post-implementation review recorded; PR/final-head CI evidence pending`; the roadmap says `blocked`. These are not the same semantic. A downstream consumer reading `spec.md` and the roadmap will see different authoritative statuses.

**This is exactly the kind of drift the slice claims to prevent.** The roadmap status was derived editorially and has not been written back to the spec files, nor do the spec files reference the roadmap as status authority.
